# Word A4 Rules

Default `output_context` is `word`.

- Assume the final image is inserted into an A4 Word report at about 5.8 inches wide.
- Keep source figures large enough that 11-12 pt body labels remain readable after insertion.
- Prefer one clear chart per image.
- Use restrained titles, axis labels, and legends.
- Do not generate chart titles with serial numbers or figure prefixes such as
  `图1`, `图2`, `Figure 1`, or `1.`. Use the actual subject title only, for
  example `济宁市2026年5月AQI与O$_3$_8H日变化趋势`. Report captions and ordering
  are handled outside the chart image.
- A chart that is readable on screen but unreadable after Word scaling is not acceptable.
